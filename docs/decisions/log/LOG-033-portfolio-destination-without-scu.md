# Name the portfolio's destination as academic and professional review, without SCU

## Decision

`CLAUDE.md` names the project's destination as a portfolio piece for academic and professional review, not tied to one institution. This replaces the item of [LOG-025](LOG-025-claude-md-wording.md) under which "SCU" was introduced at its first use; the other items of LOG-025 stand.

## Linked findings

Item N8 of the documentation-currency register; finding DC-74.

## Options considered

* (a) Introduce "SCU" at its first use, as LOG-025 set.
* (b) Name the destination without an institution.

## Option chosen

(b).

## Reasons

* The portfolio is meant for academic and professional review and is not addressed to one institution; naming one would state a destination the project does not have.

## Reasons for each rejected option

* (a): it would introduce an acronym for a destination that does not hold.

## Open, and where it goes

* ADR-0002 (`:15`) names SCU as the portfolio's destination: an addendum, in a later pull request of the same correction group.
