# Leave the voice of accepted decision records as written

## Decision

[Writing conventions, rule 2](../../writing-conventions.md#2-voice) is not applied to the text of accepted decision records. The passages of ADR-0006 at `:15`, `:16` and `:81` stay as written (DC-66).

## Linked findings

Item N9 of the [documentation-currency register](../../audit/documentation-currency.md#8-decide-items); finding DC-66.

## Options considered

* (N9a) The text left as written.
* (N9b) The passages rewritten.
* (N9c) An addendum that notes the passages.

## Option chosen

(N9a).

## Reasons

* Accepted decision records are not rewritten ([LOG-009](LOG-009-addendum-convention-home.md)); rule 2 governs text written from now on.

## Reasons for each rejected option

* (N9b): it rewrites an accepted record.
* (N9c): an addendum would correct no claim.

## Open, and where it goes

Nothing.
