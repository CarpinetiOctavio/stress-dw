# Assign the definition of the correspondence evidence document to the correspondence phase

## Decision

The name, path and contents of "the correspondence evidence document" (`CLAUDE.md:30`; ADR-0013 `:68`, `:76`) are defined in the correspondence phase. The documentation-currency sweep assigns finding DC-89 to it.

## Linked findings

Decision l of the documentation-currency register's decision list; finding DC-89.

## Options considered

* (l1) Assigned to the correspondence phase.
* (l2) Defined in this phase.

## Option chosen

(l1).

## Reasons

* The contents of the evidence document depend on the correspondence criteria, which are not yet written.
* ADR-0013 makes the merge of that document a condition for reducing the star (`:68`); its definition belongs with the criteria it reports on.

## Reasons for each rejected option

* (l2): it would fix the contents of the document before the criteria it reports on exist.

## Open, and where it goes

* Name, path and contents: the correspondence phase.
