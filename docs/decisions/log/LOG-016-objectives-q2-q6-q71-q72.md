# Set the objectives of Q2 and Q7.1 and fix the conditions for those of Q6 and Q7.2

## Decision

* Q2's objective becomes "describe how explicit stress recognition differs by gender and across the surveyed period within the subpopulation reporting a family history; no comparison group without family history, so it does not describe a relationship between family history and recognition".
* Q7.1's objective becomes "describe lifetime treatment-seeking among respondents who report 'Yes' or 'Not sure' for care options, by recognition level and inclusion condition, without implying an active decision process or a shortfall against any expected level; 'No' is outside scope".
* The objectives of Q6 and Q7.2 are written in the plan of PR-b under these conditions: Q6's names the four cuts its question names, explicit stress recognition among them, and follows the wording of [LOG-013](LOG-013-care-options-neutral-wording.md); Q7.2's uses none of "persists", "non-uptake" and "fullest context", and follows LOG-013.
* All four are recorded by a dated addendum to ADR-0004.
* A documentary verdict on whether Q2 and Q7.1 correspond to the original purpose is assigned to the correspondence phase.
* The "gap" wording of the conceptual framework (`:92`) is corrected together with these objectives; the two framings of indicator 14 (ADR-0003 `:29`, ADR-0004 `:94`) stay as written.

## Linked findings

Decision e of the [documentation-currency register](../../audit/documentation-currency.md#8-decide-items)'s decision list; findings DC-39, DC-60 and DC-76.

## Options considered

* (e1) The starting objectives of Q2 and Q7.1; conditions for Q6 and Q7.2; the documentary verdict assigned to the correspondence phase.
* (e2) All four starting objectives, and the documentary verdict, in this phase.
* (e3) The current objectives kept.

## Option chosen

(e1). PR groups: PR-a2 and PR-b.

## Reasons

* Q2's current objective compares "the co-occurrence of family history and explicit stress recognition" within a population already restricted to a family history (ADR-0004 `:58`).
* Q6's current objective omits the recognition cut its question names (ADR-0004 `:85`).
* Q7.1's current objective, "measure the gap", is contradicted by `patterns.md:45`, under which no output is stated as a treatment gap.
* Q7.2's current objective uses "persists", "non-uptake" and "fullest context" (ADR-0004 `:114`), against [writing conventions, rule 5](../../writing-conventions.md#5-names-claim-only-what-the-data-supports).
* The starting option for Q6 describes changes and gives no text, and its Q7.2 text is elided; conditions let both be written without fixing an incomplete text now.
* A verdict on correspondence with the original purpose belongs to the documentary layer of the correspondence criteria.

## Reasons for each rejected option

* (e2): Q6 and Q7.2 have no complete text to adopt, and the documentary verdict belongs to the correspondence phase.
* (e3): it keeps objectives contradicted by `patterns.md:45` and by rule 5.

## Open, and where it goes

* The objectives of Q6 and Q7.2 and the wording of the addendum: the plan of PR-b.
* The wording of the conceptual framework at `:92`: the plan of PR-a2.
* The documentary verdicts on Q2 and Q7.1: the correspondence phase.
